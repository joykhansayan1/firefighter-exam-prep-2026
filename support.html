How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app (Restore is also on the unlock screen). Use the same Apple ID (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought with.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. On Android: open Google Play, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the billing period.

Is there a free trial?
