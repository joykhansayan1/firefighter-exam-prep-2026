Summary: Firefighter Exam Prep 2026 is private by design. There is no account and no login, your study data stays on your device, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any profile. It contains no analytics or advertising software, uses no advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Study data stored on your device

Your answers, Leitner review schedule, bookmarks, mistake bank, mock-exam history, study streak, and focus sessions are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Use Settings → Reset Progress or delete the app to erase them.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android. The store handles payment under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google). We do not receive your payment details, Apple ID, or Google account; the app only checks on-device whether an active purchase exists.

4. Read-aloud

The optional read-aloud feature uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech. No audio or text is sent to any server.

5. Store privacy labels

The app is declared as collecting no data on its App Store privacy label and in its Google Play Data safety section.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a study tool for adults applying for firefighter positions and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You control all app data on your device. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
